TM2065 · SUPERVISED LEARNING · GUIDED USE-CASE WALKTHROUGHS

Supervised learning, one business story at a time

Twelve real finance use cases, each its own tab and each a different algorithm — from classification to linear regression. For every one: meet the company and the decision-maker, see and understand the labelled data, learn how the algorithm thinks in plain language (with the equation behind it explained like you’re in 6th class), watch it run step by step with the real inputs and outputs of each stage, and read the evaluation the way the decision-maker would.

Educational walkthroughs for TM2065 · Application of Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence in Finance. Every algorithm (logistic regression, decision tree, random forest, k-NN, naïve Bayes, linear SVM, one-class anomaly detection, and simple & multiple linear regression) is implemented from scratch and trained live in your browser on synthetic data.
