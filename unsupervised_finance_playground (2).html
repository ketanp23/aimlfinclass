TM2065 · UNSUPERVISED LEARNING · GUIDED USE-CASE WALKTHROUGHS

Unsupervised learning, one business story at a time

Six real finance use cases, each its own tab and each a different unsupervised algorithm — from clustering to dimensionality reduction to association rules. For every one: meet the company and the decision-maker, see and understand the unlabelled data, learn how the algorithm thinks in plain language (with the equation behind it explained like you’re in 6th class), watch it run step by step with the real inputs and outputs of each stage, and read the label-free evaluation the way the decision-maker would.

Educational walkthroughs for TM2065 · Application of Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence in Finance. Every algorithm (K-Means, DBSCAN density clustering, PCA, LDA, and Apriori-style association-rule mining) is implemented from scratch and run live in your browser on synthetic data. Because there are no labels, every evaluation metric is internal — computed from geometry, not from an answer key. Company names and personas are fictitious.
